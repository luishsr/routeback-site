Why more liquidity alone doesn't fix it

  • A cheaper fee beats a deeper pool on every normal-size trade.
  • Full-range liquidity is thin near the price; concentrated pools win with a fraction of the capital.
  • Cutting your fee only pays if your share grows enough: at half the fee you need twice the share.
  • Competing LPs follow you down. Some go to 0.3%.

FAQ

Questions

Why not just lower our pool's fee?

Sometimes that's the answer, and the routing lab will tell you. But revenue is share × fee: halve the fee and you need twice the share to break even, and competing pools can follow you down. We show the numbers before you change anything.

Who controls the liquidity?

Your treasury owns the positions. Routeback operates them within limits you set, and every action and fee split is reported.

Which pools and chains do you support?

Meteora DLMM and DAMM v2 on Solana today. Other venues follow based on demand.

Does this help referral programs?

Yes. Fees from the managed liquidity can be split into a referral and rebate share, attributed to the trades that earned them.
